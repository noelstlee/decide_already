## Still open

- The close call cutoffs. With `unsorted_top_below` 0.6 and `close_call_margin` 0.15 a close call can never happen when the probabilities sum to 1 (a top of 0.6 leaves at most 0.4 for the second, a gap of 0.2). Either lower the top cutoff under 0.575, raise the margin, or define a close call another way. Found 2026-11-15; the defaults are unchanged, and the evaluation report prints n/a for close calls for as long as they cannot fire.
- The `fits_any` cutoff. At 0.5 it sent only 8 of 17 held-out files to Unsorted; at 0.7 it would have sent all 17, and 1 of 74 others by mistake. The public set has no file that fits no group, so it cannot tune this. See [phase0-results.md](phase0-results.md).
- How much an answer moves with option order. The same question about the same files gave 6 and then 2 files under the cutoff in two runs. Not measured yet; the shuffle is not seeded.
- A drag that no sync has seen is dropped when the group it went into is renamed by hand or deleted before the next sync (the note goes back by its own group id).
- Cards whose original left the dump: the page shows them with no mark and answers 404 for every action on them.
- License for the repo. Note: PyMuPDF is AGPL-3.0 (or commercial), which constrains the choice; pypdf (BSD) is a possible swap, isolated to `extract/`.
- CLI and tool-folder names (`p0`, `.decide/`).
- Whether design docs stay public in this repo.
- New dates for the Phase 0 go/no-go (the proposed 2026-11-01 has passed) and their effect on Phase 1.
- Phase 2 choices: see the last section of [phase1.md](phase1.md).
